Model Selection Rationale

DenseNet121 achieved the highest recall, which is critical in medical diagnostic systems to minimize false negatives. While MobileNetV2 offered faster training and lower computational cost, DenseNet121 provided superior generalization performance.

Therefore, DenseNet121 was selected as the final deployment model for MalariaScanAI.
